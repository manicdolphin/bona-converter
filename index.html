How do I use this?

Select a category and two units, then type your measurement. For mixed units, try 6 ft 1 in or 14 st 3 lb. Tap any value in the comparison grid to make it the target unit. Use ☆ to save a favourite pair. The app operates offline; only settings and history are stored on this device.
